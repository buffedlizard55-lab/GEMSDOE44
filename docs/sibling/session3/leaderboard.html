How to read the gap

Through the metric's own arithmetic, a score increase at constant emitted mass is an increase in mean credit per emitted dot. The public leader (0.3345) needs, at the group's best mass (~38–44k dots), a higher hit rate than the group's best field delivers; equivalently the group's best field would need to sit higher on their top of the ranking. No public catalogue supplies that gap (the newest public compilations already lie inside the given catalogue — GEMSDOE32 measured 59,065 px, all but one within 300 m of it). The realistic routes are: (a) a better detector on the same physics (our Stage 2 — measured against arms in the holdout), (b) the two-round objective — the same file is re-scored against an expanded label set after the expert panel reviews every submission, so true-but-unlabelled faults are re-worth something here that round-1 scoring alone under-prices (α=0.2 is the organisers' own statement that false-positive mass aimed at plausible new faults is cheap), and (c) the exact post-catalogue rupture targets of G44-R2 (unbounded on the final round if verified).
