Candidate geological hypotheses — new in GEMSDOE44, ranked

Five candidates not tried in any GEMSDOE site to date. Each names the layers, the physical signature, why it catches a fault missing from the USGS/INGENIOUS catalogue rather than one already in it, and how it differs from everything already implemented (including the group's registered-but-unbuilt arms H33-1..5, H60-1..5, H61-1..3 in GEMSDOE32). Rank order = expected DTI improvement per unit implementation cost. Nothing is slot-eligible without passing the spatially-blocked holdout first (pre-registration stage2_holdout.json).

3 G44-H3
Catalogue-incompleteness relay prior: the catalogue's own uncertainty classes (Inferred / Moderately Constrained segments) and its end-to-end gaps flag where unmapped faulting is most likely		 NBMG gap links with ftype_src/tgt, gap_km, kinematic_compat; 290 relay-class links (gap < 3 km, kinematically compatible) Gaussian (σ=2 px) stamps at each gap midpoint, weight = kinematic compatibility; plus endpoint stamps weighted by the weaker segment's uncertainty class where the catalogue is uncertain or ends is exactly where unmapped faulting lives — the catalogue flags its own weak spots, and by construction those points are off the mapped line (adjacent, not on it). 4 G44-H4
Hydrothermal-plumbing composite: high conductivity × shallow conductive base × dense high heat-flow wells = active permeable structure, even without a surface scarp		 cond_surf (band 17), depth_to_base_surf (band 15), 2,108 USGS Great-Basin heat-flow well points (official GDR/ScienceBase mirror, hf_meas mW/m², 184 wells ≥ 1500) clip(z(cond),0,∞) · clip(−z(depth_to_base),0,∞) · 1[≥1 hot well within 5 km] hydrothermal systems localize on faults; conductivity marks alteration, shallow conductive base marks near-surface fluid, measured heat flow marks heat that actually reaches ground. The joint signature points at active plumbing — faults that can lack any preserved Quaternary scarp and therefore be absent from a geomorphic catalogue dilcond (GEMSDOE26, 0.1223) used conductivity alone as a coarse habitat — the failure mode we are fixing; H33-1 (wellspring geochemistry) was a removal prior and was falsified. No arm has built the 3-way joint composite at placement scale; no arm has used the heat-flow well points as a placement layer (heat flow as a basin habitat was among the worst performers, 0.0041 — the difference is 300 m placement vs basin-scale, and the holdout adjudicates) tie-break channel · cost low · data: 2 official bands + official well CSV (reprojected USAEAC_83_117 → EPSG:32611 with pyproj; 4,216 wells in grid, lat 37.40–40.72 — verified plausible) BUILT + holdout-scored channel h4_conduit; note: composite is very sparse (few pixels pass all three conditions) — RF weights it by measured importance
5 G44-H5
Seismicity lineament × magnetic edge: a linear (anisotropic) concentration of earthquake density, witnessed by a co-located magnetic edge, is an active or creeping fault that the Quaternary-surface catalogue can miss		 deq_n100a15 (band 10), ieq_n100a15 (band 16), tmi_hg (band 3) structure-tensor coherence of the seismicity-density field (σ=4 px) × positive part of z(|tmi_hg|) instrumental seismicity is a dynamic inventory: blind, creeping, and low-slip-rate faults rupture small earthquakes even when they never preserve a scarp; a geomorphic catalogue records only the last of those. Requiring the density to be linear, not a blob (tensor anisotropy) is the discriminator vs crustal noise adjacent to GEMSDOE32's registered-but-unbuilt H61-2 (seismicity tensor + det_elev curvature inflection): this variant uses a magnetic edge as the witness instead of surface curvature, and is built/measured here — disclosure included so it is not passed off as untouched territory witness channel · cost low · data: official bands on disk; free USGS ComCat hypocentres (official, API) could refine depth later — not used, not needed for this arm BUILT + holdout-scored channel h5_seis_edge (weakest measured lift — reported honestly)

Deliberately not proposed (and why)

  • Step-over / tip extension of a ridge field — GEMSDOE32 H33-5 falsified it as an addition arm (0.00767 credit/dot vs 0.05416 break-even). Our G44-H3 uses the same physics region (relay zones) but as a coarse prior channel inside a separately-validated model, not as a geometry-extension arm — the falsification does not transfer, and we say so.
  • Wellspring-chemistry removal prior — GEMSDOE32 H33-1 falsified it (10–25× too expensive per dot). Not re-proposed.
  • TDR/tilt zero-crossing on band 6 — GEMSDOE32 H61-3 rests on reading band 6 as "tilt angle"; GEMSDOE25 measured Spearman 0.9999 between band 6 and the GeoDAWN radiometric TC product. We follow the measurement (IR-44-BAND6-01); a zero-crossing transform on radiometric counts is not a magnetic TDR.
  • Catalogue-flank B=2 removal — this is a metric-theoretic consequence (hidden truth is off-catalogue by construction), not a hypothesis; it is applied deterministically after emission and disclosed as also used by the group's best file. The dot positions themselves come entirely from this system's two stages.
