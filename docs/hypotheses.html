H1 (rank 1) — Strike-frame offset-strand synthesis (anisotropic across-strike placement prior)

FALSIFIED by measurement before any submission slot

layerslabels.tif (competition catalogue) - only as a strike/geometry source; no feature band required
physical signatureOffset vector from a non-catalogue fault pixel to the nearest catalogue pixel, expressed in the local catalogue strike frame. The claim was that new geometry of an existing system sits preferentially ACROSS strike (parallel splays, relay ramps, hanging-wall strands) rather than isotropically around the trace.
why it targets catalogue-missing faultsA catalogue records where rock is exposed enough to map a trace; a parallel strand one valley-width away, or a relay ramp between two en-echelon segments, is systematically under-recorded even when the main trace is mapped.
how it differs from prior workEvery placement prior in this project family (proximity exp(-d/1km), EDGE(external), EDGE(hybrid)) is an ISOTROPIC radial kernel of distance-to-catalogue. H1 was the anisotropic alternative.
falsification testscripts/measure_population.py, registry/population.json: offset-angle histogram for SGMC-only fault pixels versus a distance-matched random footprint control, both sampled from the 1.5-9 px band from the catalogue. Result: SGMC 0.102 within 30 deg of strike / 0.736 within 30 deg of across-strike / median 74.8 deg; control 0.100 / 0.742 / 75.2 deg. Enrichment per 15 deg bin: SGMC 0.31 0.31 0.37 0.60 1.45 2.97 versus control 0.30 0.31 0.35 0.60 1.42 3.03.
verdictThe apparent across-strike preference is a distance-transform artefact (for a random point near a line, the nearest point on the line is nearly always perpendicular to it). There is NO measurable anisotropy signal beyond that geometric baseline, so the anisotropic kernel cannot be justified. Hypothesis dropped; no slot spent.
expected DTIn/a (falsified)
costlow (implemented and falsified in ~4 minutes of CPU)
data obtainabilityn/a

H2 (rank 1) — Off-catalogue supervised field: the competition catalogue is the wrong training target

IMPLEMENTED AND VALIDATED - this is the shipped field

layersall 19 official bands of training_features.tif; band 12 detrended elevation and band 19 detrended elevation slope (strongest single discriminator for off-catalogue faults); band 6 tilt angle / total curvature, band 3 TMI horizontal gradient, band 9 TMI vertical gradient, band 18 isostatic gravity horizontal gradient (potential-field edge terms); band 15 depth to basement and band 17 conductivity surface (concealment terms); bands 4, 7, 8 geodetic strain-rate tensor terms; USGS SGMC fault lines rasterised to this grid (external, public domain) used ONLY as the training target; labels.tif used ONLY as the exclusion mask and as the sanity frame, never as the training target
physical signatureSupervised P(a pixel lies on a mapped fault that the competition catalogue does not contain), learned from named multi-scale transforms (raw value, 3x3 mean, 9x9 mean, Laplacian curvature at 3 and 9 px).
why it targets catalogue-missing faultsIt is trained on real mapped faults that ARE absent from the given catalogue, so the discriminative direction it learns is the direction that separates catalogue-absent from catalogue-present terrain. Measured evidence that this matters: the discriminative SIGN of several bands flips between the two populations. Univariate AUC for detrended_elevation_slope_m3 is 0.747 for SGMC-only faults but only 0.561 for catalogue faults; geodetic_2nd_invariant is 0.478 for SGMC-only versus 0.560 for catalogue. A model trained on the catalogue therefore learns partly the wrong direction.
how it differs from prior workPrior repositories in this family (GEMSDOE19/24/25/27/28/32/33) rank and promote candidates on frames whose truth IS the catalogue, and GEMSDOE32 measured that such frames rank artifacts in the OPPOSITE order to the live board (0.17193 > 0.16635 > 0.16177 catalogue-proxy versus live 0.1922 < 0.2477 < 0.2600). This repo trains the field itself on the off-catalogue population and promotes only on that frame. GEMSDOE29 registered an 'sgmc-off-catalogue-44k' artifact but never obtained a score for it; no prior repo reports a spatially blocked off-catalogue AUC or uses that frame for model selection.
falsification testnot applicable
verdictpending
expected DTIpositive; measured on the off-catalogue frame in registry/holdout.json
costmedium (CPU only, ~15 minutes per 4-fold run on 2 vCPU)
data obtainabilitySGMC raster is obtainable and hash-verified in this sandbox via `gh api repos/buffedlizard55-lab/GEMSDOE24/contents/data/external/derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif` (198,602 B, sha256 643cbe992ef4ba37...).

H3 (rank 2) — Metric-exact marginal-credit emission at the analytic mass

IMPLEMENTED - shipped inside the H2 artifact

layersnone: this is metric arithmetic, not data
physical signatureNot a physical signature. The official metric's TP term is a MAX over a 300 m disc, so expected credit is submodular in the emitted set and fixed-order greedy carries the standard (1 - 1/e) guarantee. The first-order stopping condition is k > alpha * DTI, i.e. a dot pays iff its kernel credit exceeds 20 percent of the current score.
why it targets catalogue-missing faults
how it differs from prior workGEMSDOE30/32 implemented an expected-marginal-credit emitter (EDGE) but only ever swept the thinning radius at 1.5 and 2.8 px and reported the emission side as 'nearly used up'. This repo emits on a field whose belief is an off-catalogue probability, and derives the mass from leave-one-fold-out selection on the off-catalogue frame plus the closed-form break-even condition.
falsification testnot applicable
verdictpending
expected DTI+0.005 to +0.025 on the off-catalogue frame versus a raster-order thinning cascade at matched mass, mechanism measured in registry/holdout.json
costlow
data obtainabilityn/a

H4 (rank 3) — Coupled ridge-trough (curvature dipole) matched filter on 1 m lidar

REGISTERED - NOT RUN; data partially obtainable

layersUSGS 3DEP 1 m DEM tiles (external); the owner-derived derivative raster data/external/lidar_scarp_features_u8.tif (36,943,606 B, hash-pinned in the GEMSDOE24 mirror)
physical signatureA fault scarp is a step in the surface whose second derivative is a paired ridge and trough; the separation between the pair encodes scarp width and height. A matched filter for that dipole, at multiple scales and azimuths with strike-consistency gating, detects scarps whose wavelength is below the 100 m competition grid.
why it targets catalogue-missing faultsThe metric's FP tax is 0.2 per unit while FN costs 0.8, so a scarp that is real but subtle is worth predicting. A 5 m scarp over 20 m of ground is invisible in a 100 m band but is exactly the kind of feature an expert adds when they work from lidar.
how it differs from prior workGEMSDOE7 shipped a lidar-scarp ranking at 0.1461 and GEMSDOE24 stores the derived scarp raster, but no prior repo in this family runs a coupled ridge-trough dipole detector with strike-consistency gating and validates it on the off-catalogue frame.
falsification testnot applicable
verdictpending
expected DTIpositive but unmeasured here; the field would be added as an extra feature to H2
costmedium-high (multi-scale filtering over a 36.9 MB derived raster plus 1 m tile mosaicking)
data obtainabilityThe derived scarp raster IS obtainable from this sandbox via the hash-pinned mirror. The raw 3DEP 1 m tiles are NOT reachable from this sandbox: prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com and earthexplorer.usgs.gov both returned HTTP 000 (connection failure) on a direct test on 2026-10-06.

H7 (rank 1) — Exact greedy max-coverage emission at the metric's own spacing (adjacent dots are forbidden)

under test

layersall 19 official bands (the field it is applied to)
physical signaturemetric arithmetic: a dot's credit is max_x k(d(x,g)); a dot 8-adjacent to another adds ~no new truth coverage while each dot pays alpha=0.2 of FP.
why it targets catalogue-missing faultsIt is an emission rule, not a field: it decides WHERE mass goes without changing what is believed, and dots on catalogue pixels are deleted by the mask, so it can only earn credit on unmapped truth.
how it differs from prior workThe family decimated rasters to reach 44,090 dots (d2.8 thinning, Poisson); this solves the coverage problem directly by marginal expected credit on the accepted set, and verifies zero 8-adjacency in the shipped file.
falsification testShip or score it against the incumbent at matched density on the blocked folds; check 8-adjacency = 0 and that it never loses to a raster-prefix emission.
verdictSupported descriptively at n=19: the three best reported artifacts have 0.001-0.002 adjacent-dot fractions vs 0.88-0.99 for low scorers; Spearman(live, adjacent fraction) = -0.361.
expected DTIadds the difference between a clumped and a spaced emission of the same field; on the family's own history that difference was 0.26 vs 0.19 at 2.75x the mass.
costlow (already implemented; seconds per emission)
data obtainabilityn/a
